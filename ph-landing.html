Features

Everything your clipboard should be

XCopy keeps a full history of everything you copy, so you never lose a snippet again — all in a tiny Windows-native package.

📋

Automatic History

Every text and image you copy is automatically recorded in the background. No manual saving, no special shortcuts — it just works.

🔍

Instant Search

Find anything you copied hours or days ago in milliseconds. Fuzzy search across hundreds of entries as you type.

🖼

Image Preview

Copy screenshots, diagrams, or photos — XCopy stores and displays image previews inline so you can identify them at a glance.

⌨

Double Ctrl to Paste

Double-tap the Ctrl key to pop open the history window. Select any entry and it pastes instantly — zero friction, maximum speed.

⌨️

Customizable Hotkeys

Set your own global shortcuts for showing history, pasting specific entries, or clearing the clipboard. Maps to whatever feels natural.

💾

SQLite Local Storage

Your clipboard history lives in a local SQLite database — zero telemetry, no cloud sync, no accounts. Your data stays yours.
